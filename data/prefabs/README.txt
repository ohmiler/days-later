Building layouts, one file each. The city picks one that fits each plot.

The first lines are settings:
  kinds: shop            which buildings may use it (shop, store)
Then the plan, as seen from above, back at the top and the street at the
bottom. Every row is the same width (the building's width). A row that
starts with ~ can be repeated to make the building deeper (the ~ is not part
of the row); the plan itself must be no deeper than the shallowest plot (13).

  W wall            . floor
  D front door (bottom row)          B back door (top row; a wall if nothing is behind)
  d door inside the building          w shop window in the front wall (sometimes wall)
Furniture you can search (it blocks the cell):
  f fridge   c cabinet   s shelf   k counter   t table   x crate
  b bed (b with another b under it is a full-length bed)
  ? whatever the shop sells from (the shop's own furniture: shelves, fridges...)
Things on the floor (no blocking):
  S stairs to the roof   m mattress   v tv   n fan   h spirit shrine
  o boxes   p noodle pot   r chairs   i tyres   e motorbike   l oil drums
Things with a state (see Things):
  T a tap (7 in 10 have one)   R a radio (about 1 in 5)

Rooms with a bed are the family's home and hold what homes hold; the room with
the front door holds what the shop sells. A layout without beds is all shop.
